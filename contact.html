Contact FAQ

How quickly do you respond to pitches?
We aim to respond to every pitch within 5 working days. If you haven’t heard back after a week, feel free to send a gentle follow-up.
Do you accept guest posts?
We rarely accept unsolicited guest posts, but we do commission articles from architects, designers, and researchers. If you have a unique perspective and strong writing samples, send us a pitch.
Can I republish an article from Architectural Line Theory?
Short excerpts (up to 100 words) with a clear link back to the original are permitted for non-commercial use. For full republishing, please contact us for permission.
How do I submit a correction?
Email editors@archline.theory with the subject line “Correction” and the article URL. We review all corrections promptly.
Do you have a physical address?
We are a digital-first publication with editors based in Milan, London, and New York. Our registered correspondence address is: Architectural Line Theory, Via Brera 12, 20121 Milano, Italy.
