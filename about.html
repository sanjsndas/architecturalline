Architectural Line Theory is an independent journal for people who care about the built environment — and the quiet ways it shapes our lives.
Founded 2019 · Independent & reader-supported · Published weekly

Our mission

We started Architectural Line Theory because we were tired of design coverage that felt either superficial or needlessly academic. There is a middle ground: rigorous, curious, and deeply human. We write for architects, interior designers, students, and anyone who has ever paused in a doorway to notice how a room makes them feel.

Our editorial stance is simple: good design is not about trends. It is about thoughtfulness, longevity, and the experience of the people who inhabit a space. We favour long-form essays over listicles, measured drawings over mood boards, and honest critique over press releases.

“The plan is the generator. It is the invisible architecture that holds everything together.” — Le Corbusier (paraphrased)

Every week we publish at least one substantial article — a deep dive into a material, a case study of a building, or a practical guide to lighting, layout, or restoration. We also run interviews with makers, architects, and thinkers who are shaping the built environment in quiet, meaningful ways.

What we cover

  • Interior design — spatial planning, colour theory, furniture, and the psychology of rooms.
  • Architecture — critical essays on buildings, urbanism, and the social role of design.
  • Materials — from lime plaster to cross-laminated timber, we track how things are made and how they age.
  • Lighting — the most underrated tool in a designer’s kit. We cover daylight, electric light, and everything in between.
  • Small spaces — compact living, multifunctional furniture, and the art of doing more with less.
  • Sustainability — not as a buzzword, but as a practical, material reality.

We are not affiliated with any manufacturer, showroom, or design school. Our recommendations are based on research and first-hand experience.

Editorial policy

Our journalism is independent and reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored posts, native advertising, or paid product placement. Occasionally we use affiliate links — these are clearly marked, and they never influence what we choose to write about.

Corrections: We aim for accuracy, but we are human. If you spot an error, please contact us and we will correct it promptly and transparently.

Privacy: We collect only the data we need to run the site and send our newsletter. We never sell or share your information. Read our full privacy policy.

AI policy: We do not publish AI-generated articles. All text is written by humans, and all images are either licensed or created by our team.

Why independent?

Most design media is funded by advertising from the very companies it covers. That creates a quiet conflict of interest: it’s hard to write critically about a furniture brand when they’re paying your bills. Architectural Line Theory is funded entirely by reader subscriptions and the occasional grant. That means we can say what we think — and we can take the time to get things right.

If you value what we do, please consider subscribing to our newsletter or sharing an article with a friend. It makes a real difference.
