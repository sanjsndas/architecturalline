Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple and transparent.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third-party sources.

Information you provide directly

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: email address and optional name.
  • Comments and feedback: any information you choose to share in public discussions or direct correspondence.
  • Pitch submissions: writing samples, portfolio links, and biographical information.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on pages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see our Cookies Policy for full details.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent, trade, or sell your email address or any other personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries: replying to contact form submissions, pitches, and correction requests.
  • To send newsletters: if you have opted in, we use your email to send editorial updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve our content: analysing which articles are read and how visitors navigate the site helps us make better editorial decisions.
  • To maintain security: detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations: when required by applicable law.

3. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences, and understand how our content is used.

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Necessary for the site to function — security, form submission, consent preferences. Session / up to 1 year
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with the site (e.g., page views, traffic sources). Data is aggregated and anonymised where possible. Up to 2 years
Preferences Remember settings such as dark mode or language. Up to 1 year

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

4. Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the publication. These providers may process your data only as necessary to provide their services:

  • Email delivery: newsletter and transactional emails.
  • Analytics: privacy-focused website analytics.
  • Content delivery: hosting and CDN services for fast, reliable access.
  • Form handling: secure processing of contact form submissions.

Each provider is bound by data processing agreements and is required to protect your information in accordance with applicable law.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Contact form submissions: retained for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period for suppression lists.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregated form for up to 26 months.
  • Legal records: retained as required by applicable law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Restriction: request that we limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Objection: object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@archline.theory. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Architectural Line Theory is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International transfers

Our editorial team is based in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and the United States. If you are located outside these regions, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries that may have different data protection laws.

Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure your data receives adequate protection.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or through a notice on the site.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
